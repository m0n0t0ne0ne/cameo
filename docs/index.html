CAMEO.

An edge-bar app launcher that opens up to 10 apps as floating pop-up windows.

Download APK See how it works Setup guide

A TOUR

Every clip below was recorded on a Nothing Phone running Cameo 0.8.0.

01 · THE DOCK

Tap the edge, pick an app, it floats.

A slim five-dot bar sits on the edge of the screen. Tap it and the dock slides out with up to ten apps. Tap one, and a card grows from its icon into a Nothing OS pop-up window, right on top of whatever you were doing.

  • Pick another app and the pop-up switches over cleanly
  • Long-press an icon to open it the other way (full screen or pop-up), or to close it
  • With Shizuku, the old pop-up goes to the background instead of turning into a Nothing bubble
02 · ANY EDGE

Put the bar where your thumb is.

Drag the bar up or down, or across to the other side of the screen. It snaps to the nearest edge, and the dock opens from that side. Cameo remembers where you left it, and starts again after a reboot.

  • Left or right edge, any height
  • If the dock is taller than the screen, the icons scroll
  • Hidden on the lock screen
03 · MAKE IT YOURS

Settings that change the bar as you tap.

Every change shows on the bar straight away. Pick a colour: black, graphite, white, red, or one taken from your wallpaper. Set the opacity, and choose Nothing-themed icons, the originals, or an installed icon pack.

  • Open apps as a pop-up window or full screen
  • Long press to close an app, or open it the other way
  • Reorder the dock by dragging, or with the arrows
  • Swipe an app left to take it off the dock, with Undo
  • Search all your installed apps and pick up to ten

WHAT YOU NEED

Cameo is built around Nothing OS's own pop-up view.

Nothing Phone

Nothing OS with pop-up view, on Android 12 (API 31) or newer. Tested on Nothing OS 5 (Android 17).

Accessibility

Draws the bar over other apps without a notification. Cameo never reads the screen.

Shizuku (optional)

Shizuku lets Cameo manage pop-ups properly: no Nothing bubbles, and ✕ really closes the app.

SET IT UP

Cameo isn't on the Play Store. Download the APK from GitHub and install it on your phone. Setup takes about five minutes, or two without Shizuku.

Install

  1. Download the APK on your phone. Tap Download APK, or get it from the Releases page.
  2. Allow your browser to install apps. Open the downloaded file. If Android says installing unknown apps isn't allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Install it. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unrecognised app, tap More details › Install anyway.
  4. Open Cameo and pick your apps. Choose up to ten, then tap Done. Cameo asks once about notifications. You can say no if you'll use the accessibility service below.

Turn on the bar

  1. Turn on Cameo's accessibility service. In Cameo, tap Accessibility settings, or go to Settings › Accessibility › Installed apps › Cameo, and switch it on. Cameo's settings should then say Accessibility overlay: on. No notifications.
  2. If the switch is greyed out ("Restricted setting"). Android blocks this for apps installed from a download. Go to Settings › Apps › Cameo, tap ⋮ in the top corner, choose Allow restricted settings and confirm with your PIN. Then go back and turn the service on.
  3. Shortcut with Shizuku. Once Shizuku is connected (see below), tap Remove notifications under Bar mode in Cameo. Cameo switches its own accessibility service on, with no trip to Settings and no restricted-setting step. Both overlay notifications disappear.
  4. No accessibility? Use the fallback. Tap Allow "Display over other apps" in Cameo and turn it on for Cameo. The bar works the same, but Android shows a permanent notification. Tap Hide notification in Cameo to switch that notification off.

Connect Shizuku (optional)

  1. Install Shizuku from the Play Store or GitHub.
  2. Turn on Developer options. Go to Settings › About phone and tap Build number seven times. Then turn on Settings › System › Developer options › Wireless debugging. You need to be on Wi-Fi.
  3. Pair and start Shizuku. In Shizuku, tap Pairing under Start via Wireless debugging. In Wireless debugging, tap Pair device with pairing code and type the code into Shizuku's notification. Then tap Start in Shizuku.
  4. Let Cameo use it. In Cameo's Shizuku section, tap Allow, then Allow all the time. It should say Connected.
  5. After a restart. Without root, Shizuku stops whenever the phone restarts. Open Shizuku and tap Start again. Until you do, Cameo keeps working without the Shizuku extras.

Build it yourself

You need JDK 17+ and an Android SDK with platform 36.

git clone https://github.com/m0n0t0ne0ne/cameo.git
cd cameo
./gradlew assembleRelease
adb install -r app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk
